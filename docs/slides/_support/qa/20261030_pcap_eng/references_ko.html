Two issues in CSV-based IDS classification
Issue 1 Early saturation in some attacks
Head attacks such as DDoS and Bot reach F1 in the 90% range with n = 2 to 20 → a high data ceiling before adding labels
Issue 2 Identical features, different labels
Rows with dozens of identical flow statistics can still have different recorded labels → one deterministic classifier cannot fit both
Early saturation and feature–label contradictions require separate diagnosis
